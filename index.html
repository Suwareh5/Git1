    This is me

    I'm an Interaction Design student at Linnaeus University in Sweden. I enjoy combining creativity and problem-solving to develop digital experiences that are both visually engaging and easy to use. Outside my studies, I enjoy drawing and exploring visual ideas. I’m curious about how people think, what influences their decisions and how design can make everyday interactions better.

    My memories

    I have a memory from when I was in 7th grade. I was sitting in my classroom with my teacher and some of my friends, I pulled out my phone to show them a song that I thought was funny but also catchy. I started singing along with it as well, then I strated to notice more and more poeple gather around us, even people from other classes came to watch. Soon I was performing infront of many students and teachers. It was a fun memory for me and I wish it was recorded, but at least I remember it.

    My interests

    I draw a lot of manga on my freetime. When I was younger, I used to watch a lot of anime and read manga, I wasn't so good at drawing yet but I decided to practise because I had a goal. I wanted people to feel joy and exitment reading my manga the same way that I did from reading the books when I was younger. I started practising and have now become a pretty good artist who is working on his own manga.

    My work

    GatherRound

    A concept exploring how friends can watch videos together remotely. The project focused on creating a shared experience that combines video viewing with social interaction.

    • Developed user scenarios and design concepts
    • Designed interface elements and interactive features

    Moodle - Redesign

    A project part of a university course focused on participatory and user-centered design. The aim was to explore factors that decrease student motivation and explore different design solutions.

    • Translate user research into interaction design decisions
    • Work iteratively with prototyping tools like Figma

    Kalmar kommun

    A website redesign project focused on improving how users navigate and access information. The project involved considering user needs and creating a clearer interface.

    • Developed visual design solutions
    • Refined the interface with usability in mind
